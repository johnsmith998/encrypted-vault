## English

### Overview

**Encrypted Vault** is **dual-licensed**:

| License | Who | Cost |
|---------|-----|------|
| [MIT](LICENSE) | Individuals, learning, open-source projects, internal evaluation | Free (keep copyright + MIT notice) |
| **Commercial License** | Organizations with the use cases below | Contact for quote |

You may use the software under **MIT** for many scenarios without contacting us.
The **Commercial License** is for organizations that need a **written agreement**,
**support**, **indemnification**, or clarity beyond the MIT “AS IS” terms.

### When to contact us (Commercial License)

Please **open an Issue (label `commercial`) or email** before commercial
deployment if any of the following apply:

1. **OEM / white-label** — bundling these scripts (or derivatives) into a
   **paid product** or service marketed to third parties under your brand.
2. **SaaS or managed service** — offering vault creation/management to
   customers as part of a hosted or multi-tenant product.
3. **Enterprise rollout** — company-wide deployment requiring **SLA**, training,
   customization, or a **signed agreement**.
4. **Indemnification** — you need **warranty or liability coverage** not
   provided under MIT.
5. **Trademark** — use of project name/branding in a way that implies
   endorsement (MIT does not grant trademark rights).

### What the Commercial License can include

- Non-exclusive commercial use rights (scope defined in agreement)
- Optional priority support or implementation assistance
- Optional indemnification / warranty terms (negotiated)
- Invoice / contract suitable for procurement

Pricing and terms are **negotiated per customer**. There is no self-serve checkout yet.

### MIT still applies unless you sign Commercial License

If you use the software **only under MIT**, you do **not** need a Commercial
License for typical personal or internal scripting use, provided you comply
with the MIT notice requirements in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

### 建议/contact 取得商业许可的场景

若符合以下 **任一** 情况，请在使用或对外发布前通过 **Issue（标签 `commercial`）或邮件** 联系：

1. **OEM / 白标** — 将本脚本（或衍生版本）打包进 **对外销售的收费产品或服务**。
2. **SaaS / 托管服务** — 作为多租户或云端产品的一部分，向客户提供「保险库创建/管理」能力。
3. **企业批量部署** — 全公司推广，需要 **SLA、培训、定制** 或 **正式合同**。
4. **免责/质保** — 需要 MIT 未提供的 **质保或责任限制** 条款。
5. **商标** — 以可能暗示官方背书的方式使用项目名称或品牌（MIT 不授予商标权）。

### 商业许可可包含的内容

- 非独占商业使用权（范围在协议中约定）
- 可选优先支持或实施协助
- 可选 indemnification / 质保条款（面议）
- 适合企业采购的合同与发票

价格与条款 **按客户面议**，暂无自助购买页面。

### 未签商业协议时

若你 **仅在 MIT 条款下** 使用（保留 LICENSE 中的版权声明），一般 **无需** 另行购买商业许可，例如个人电脑上的脚本使用、内部脚本评估等。
